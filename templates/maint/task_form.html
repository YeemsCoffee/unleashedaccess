A task on equipment is wherever the equipment is installed, and moves with it. A task list gives the steps to tick off when it's done (task lists).

When it's due

By time (every so many days), by usage on one of its equipment's counters (e.g. every 10,000 shots), or both: it's due at whichever comes first. A usage-based task's date is estimated from the counter's readings. {% if not counters %}Add counters on an equipment page to use them here.{% endif %}

Part

With a lead time, the part is listed under Parts to order on the Maintenance overview that many days before the task is due.
