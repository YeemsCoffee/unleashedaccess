Locations counted as inventory

COGS is last month's ending inventory, plus purchases received, minus inventory now. Inventory is the stock on hand in the locations ticked here (for example the main warehouse, cold storage and Gardena); stock that leaves them counts as used. Tick none to count every warehouse.

Unleashed's stock "as at" a date

COGS takes the stock at the end of last month and now. Depending on how Unleashed counts, its stock "as at Oct 31" is the stock at the end of Oct 31, or at its start (before that day's sales and receipts). Read the wrong way, a day's sales land in the wrong month: one month's "not on an order or write-off" goes negative and the next one's positive.
