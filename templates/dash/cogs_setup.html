Locations counted as inventory

COGS is last month's ending inventory, plus purchases received, minus inventory now. Inventory is the stock on hand in the locations ticked here (for example the main warehouse, cold storage and Gardena); stock that leaves them counts as used. Tick none to count every warehouse.

When Unleashed's stock "as at" a date is

COGS needs the stock at midnight at the end of last month and of this one. Unleashed gives stock "as at" a date, which can be the stock at the end of that day or its start, in this business's time zone or in another (UTC, or New Zealand, where Unleashed is based). Core takes the date nearest midnight and adds or takes away the documents in between, so a month's sales all land in it. Read the wrong way, sales near midnight land in the wrong month and show up under "not on an order or write-off": one month negative, the next positive.

Charges on purchase orders

Unleashed's API can't add cost lines to a purchase order, so freight, fuel surcharges and other charges made in Core go on as lines on a charge product: one made in Unleashed for it, not stock-tracked (e.g. FREIGHT, FUEL-SURCHARGE). Tick those products: the purchase order form offers them under Charges, and COGS counts their lines with shipping, not as stock bought. Products coded FREIGHT, SHIPPING, DELIVERY, FUEL, FUEL-SURCHARGE or SURCHARGE count anyway.
