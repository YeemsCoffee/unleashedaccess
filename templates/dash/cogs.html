COGS vs cost of sales — what's the difference?

COGS (the big number) is the inventory-value method: last month's ending inventory, plus everything received this month including shipping cost lines, minus what's on hand now. It captures every reason stock value left: items sold, used in drinks, spoiled, wasted, or lost in a stocktake.

Cost of sales only counts stock that went out on completed sales orders, valued at Unleashed's average landed cost at the time of sale (which already includes shipping). Write-offs are stock adjustments and stock counts (done in Unleashed or in Core) that reduced inventory value.

The two should land close together. Unleashed records every change to stock on a document, so a gap means a document Core reads differently from Unleashed (dated another day, costs changed after receipt) or one it doesn't read. "Not on an order or write-off" shows each product's working, day by day.
