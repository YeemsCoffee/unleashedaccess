Sign-in isn't set up yet

This dashboard shows your real Unleashed data, so it only opens for people who sign in.

To turn on sign-in, add these settings and restart the app (on Elastic Beanstalk: Configuration → Updates, monitoring, and logging → Environment properties → Apply):

  • ADMIN_EMAIL: your email
  • ADMIN_PASSWORD: a temporary password, at least 10 characters

Running it on a private computer nobody else can reach? ALLOW_NO_SIGN_IN=1 in .env skips sign-in.
